# Pusula blog — venue first

User rejected the quiet editorial direction. No serif typefaces. The restaurant is dense, colourful and sociable: floral ceiling, yellow chairs, green walls and red checked tables. The homepage's actual photography, sunflower divider and cloth asset are the shared visual authority.

Typography is exclusively Source Sans 3, self-hosted with Turkish glyphs. Display and article headings are bold, close and direct; body text stays clear and open. No ornamented serif masthead, no beige luxury-magazine composition.

Index: forest-green introduction joined to a large photograph of the actual restaurant. Sunflowers bridge the photograph to a checked-table archive. Images and paper copy areas form a substantial lead story followed by subject-specific stories. Filters distinguish food, the neighbourhood and evenings.

Articles: bold cream headings and yellow category labels on forest green, a large unique subject photograph, then a quiet reading column with navigable contents. Checked fabric returns at the opening hem and onward links. Sources stay readable. The venue’s visual density stays in the frame, not behind body text.

Desktop and mobile preserve the same palette and material identity. Mobile places the subject photograph directly after the heading; navigation and contents use native expandable controls. Movement uses short transform/opacity transitions and respects reduced motion; there are no continuous reading distractions.
