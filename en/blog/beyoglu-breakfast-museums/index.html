After Breakfast in Beyoğlu: Salt, Pera and Istanbul Modern

After breakfast in Beyoğlu, choose one cultural stop rather than trying to cover the whole city. Staying on İstiklal, visiting a museum on Meşrutiyet Caddesi and continuing to Tophane are three different plans. Here is how to pair your breakfast at Pusula Lounge with the place you actually want to visit.

Choose your visit day before your breakfast time

Pusula’s published breakfast service is daily 08:00–17:00. Cultural venues follow their own schedules: Pera Museum and Istanbul Modern list Monday closures on their official visit pages. Salt Beyoğlu lists visiting days from Tuesday to Sunday. Check the venue’s visit page first, then choose the date and time for your breakfast request. A restaurant being open does not establish that a museum is open too.

Stay on İstiklal: Salt Beyoğlu

Salt Beyoğlu is at İstiklal Caddesi 136. Salt Galata is a different building at a different address, so check which one you are opening on the map. The official visit page lists Tuesday–Saturday 11:00–19:00, Sunday 11:00–18:00 and free admission for Salt Beyoğlu. Those details were checked on 28 October 2026; use Salt’s own page for the current programme and holiday exceptions. This is an option for keeping your post-breakfast visit around İstiklal.

A museum on Meşrutiyet Caddesi: Pera Museum

Pera Museum is at Meşrutiyet Caddesi No:65 in Tepebaşı, Beyoğlu. Its official page lists Tuesday–Saturday 10:00–19:00, Friday until 22:00 and Sunday 12:00–18:00. If you choose an early Sunday breakfast, account for the museum opening at noon. Select the current exhibition and check ticket conditions on the museum’s own website. Your breakfast and museum visit are separate arrangements; Pusula does not sell a combined ticket or package.

Continue to Tophane: Istanbul Modern

Istanbul Modern’s official address is Tophane İskele Caddesi No:1/1, Beyoğlu. It lists Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Saturday and Sunday 10:00–18:00, with Friday 10:00–20:00; entry ends 30 minutes before closing. Tophane tram station is among the transport options on the museum’s official page. Choose this as a separate waterfront stop rather than treating it as the same location as the İstiklal venues. Check the route and travel time for your own starting point.

Keep breakfast, transport and admission separate

Pusula’s menu lists separate total prices for shared Van breakfast portions for two, three and four guests. Solo visitors can compare individual plates and hot dishes. Transport and museum admission are separate from the breakfast price. Do not assume a venue’s free or discounted entry applies to every visitor: age, student status or residency conditions may apply. Check the current conditions with the institution before relying on a concession.

Pick the stop that fits your day

Start with breakfast and one cultural venue, leaving room for the rest of your day. Compare Salt Beyoğlu for an İstiklal stop, Pera for its Meşrutiyet address and Istanbul Modern for continuing towards Tophane. If travelling with a stroller, a group or specific mobility needs, confirm access with both Pusula and the venue. These are independent visit suggestions, not a guided tour or an affiliation with the institutions.
