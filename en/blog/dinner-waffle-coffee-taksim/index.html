Dinner, Waffles and a Coffee Break in Taksim

Planning an evening near İstiklal? Start with the kind of meal or break you want, then decide how much time to leave at the table. Pusula Lounge serves food as well as coffee and desserts beyond breakfast. This guide helps you explore the evening options in Taksim and check the details that matter before you visit.

Choose the kind of dinner you have in mind

Pusula Lounge’s website highlights pizza, burgers and fish in its food section, alongside salads. Ask the team which dishes, portions and prices are current; do not assume every item is available in the same way every day. If your group wants different things, discuss the options together before ordering.

Leave room for waffles and dessert

You can finish dinner with something sweet or stop by just for a waffle. Pusula Lounge offers waffles and desserts. Check the menu or ask the team about current varieties, accompaniments and prices before ordering, so your choice reflects what is actually being served that day.

Make time for tea or coffee

If you are exploring Beyoğlu without planning a full dinner, you can also stop in for tea or coffee. Ask the team about the current coffee and tea selection, serving options and prices. If you plan to share a dessert with your drinks, confirm the portion and accompaniments before ordering.

Plan your evening visit

Pusula Lounge is at Katip Mustafa Çelebi Mahallesi, Küçük Parmak Kapı Sokak, Sinek Çıkmazı No:3 in Beyoğlu. Use the map link to check the door number; call +90 507 816 29 94 if you have trouble finding the entrance. General opening hours and breakfast service are different: breakfast is listed daily from 08:00 to 17:00. Confirm evening table availability and the menu for your preferred time with the team.

Confirm your table request on WhatsApp

Enter your date, time and guest count in the reservation form, then send the request through WhatsApp. Your table is reserved when the team confirms availability; completing the form alone does not confirm a booking. Add a celebration, high-chair request or other need to the note and wait for confirmation. You can use the same form for a small group, dinner or a waffle stop.
