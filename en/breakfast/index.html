Late breakfast in Taksim: plan your visit

Looking for Turkish breakfast after noon? Pusula Lounge serves breakfast every day from 08:00 to 17:00 near İstiklal Avenue in Beyoğlu. These breakfast hours also apply on weekends; check special-day hours and availability with our team.

The venue is open 24 hours, but breakfast is served only during the breakfast hours above. If you are arriving close to 17:00, ask the team about the last breakfast order before setting off.

Visiting on your own?

The individual breakfast plate is TRY 500 and the pişi plate is TRY 350 in our web menu. The shared Van breakfast has portions for 2, 3 or 4 people. Choose an individual plate if you are not looking for a shared spread.
