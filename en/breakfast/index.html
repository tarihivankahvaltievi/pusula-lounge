Choosing your breakfast

The shared breakfast lists menemen and pan-fried eggs together with cheeses, olives and accompaniments. For a separate hot dish, browse the gözleme, menemen and omelette categories. Individual menu items are listed separately from the shared breakfast; ask our team about additions and serving arrangements.

The menu contains dairy, eggs and wheat-based dishes. A menu description is not a full allergen list. For allergies, vegan or gluten-free needs, confirm ingredients and cross-contact with the team before ordering.
