Before you come

When is my reservation confirmed?

The form prepares a WhatsApp message with your details. Send it in WhatsApp; your reservation is confirmed when our team confirms table availability.

What are the breakfast hours?

Breakfast is served daily from 08:00 to 17:00. Ask our team about special-day hours and table availability at your preferred time.

How do I request a group table or special arrangements?

Enter your guest count and add high-chair requests, allergies or other needs in the note. Please confirm with our team that we can accommodate them.

Can I request a table for dinner, waffles or breakfast?

Yes. Choose your date, time and guest count, then send a table request through WhatsApp. Breakfast items and portion prices are listed on our website menu. Ask the team to confirm evening dishes, waffles, desserts and current prices for your visit. Your reservation is confirmed when the team confirms table availability.
