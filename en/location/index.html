Travel time and fare depend on your starting point, connections and service conditions. Confirm station and restaurant access before travelling with a stroller or specific mobility requirements.

By metro: M2 → Taksim
Use Taksim station on the M2 Yenikapı–Hacıosman line. After leaving the station, open walking directions to Pusula Lounge’s full address. Choose your exit using the current signs and map; temporary exit closures can change the final route.
From Sultanahmet or Karaköy: T1 → Kabataş → F1 → Taksim
Take the T1 towards Kabataş, then change at Kabataş to the F1 Taksim–Kabataş funicular. F1 connects with M2 at Taksim. Allow for the interchange, waiting time and the final walk when planning your breakfast.
From the İstiklal area: the final walk
Set your destination to the restaurant rather than İstiklal Avenue alone. Use Pusula Lounge, Katip Mustafa Çelebi Mahallesi, Küçük Parmak Kapı Sokak, Sinek Çıkmazı No:3. If you cannot find the entrance, call us on +90 507 816 29 94.
