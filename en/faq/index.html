Where is Pusula Lounge?

Katip Mustafa Çelebi Mahallesi, Küçük Parmak Kapı Sokak, Sinek Çıkmazı No:3, Beyoğlu, Istanbul.

What are the breakfast service hours?

Breakfast service: Every day 08:00–17:00. General opening hours: Open 24 hours every day. Being open overnight does not mean breakfast is served all night. Confirm special-day schedules by phone.

How many people can share Van breakfast?

The menu lists portions for 2 people, 3 people, 4 people. Each portion has its own price; it is not automatically a per-person charge.

What is included in shared Van breakfast?

Cheeses, honey and clotted cream, olives, tahini with grape molasses, jam, tomato, cucumber, menemen, pan-fried eggs and unlimited tea are listed in the shared breakfast. See the menu for the complete description.

Which breakfasts include tea?

Shared Van breakfast lists unlimited tea. The individual breakfast plate lists tea; the pişi plate does not. Ask the team about additional service terms before ordering.

Where can I read the menu prices?

Portions and Turkish lira (₺ / TRY) prices appear in the breakfast menu. Confirm prices and availability before ordering.

What can an individual guest choose?

The breakfast plate and pişi plate are separate dishes. Gözleme, menemen and kaşar omelette are also listed separately. Compare their contents and prices in the menu.

Does the website form book a table?

Send your request through the reservation form on WhatsApp and wait for the team to confirm your table.

How can I check table availability?

Call +905078162994 with your date, time and group size. A request becomes confirmed only after the team accepts it.

How should I discuss allergies or dietary needs?

Menu descriptions are not complete allergen lists. Ask the team about ingredients, preparation and cross-contact before ordering. Dish names alone do not establish vegan or gluten-free suitability.
